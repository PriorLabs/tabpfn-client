Add `presets` to `TabPFNClassifier` and `TabPFNRegressor`, named bundles of `inference_config` values. `TabPFNRegressor(presets="zero_inflated")` is for targets with many exact zeros, and `"grouped"` pools the predictions of a group. Presets combine, as in `["zero_inflated", "grouped"]`. The classifier offers only `"grouped"`.
